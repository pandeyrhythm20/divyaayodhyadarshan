Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Divya Ayodhya Darshan. By booking a pilgrimage package with us, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Booking and Confirmation

All bookings are subject to availability and confirmation of payment. A booking is only considered confirmed once the full payment is successfully processed via our secure payment gateway (Razorpay).

2. Travel Documents

It is the responsibility of the pilgrim to carry valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, etc.) during the entire journey. Without valid ID, temple entry may be restricted, and Divya Ayodhya Darshan will not be liable for refunds.

3. Itinerary Changes

We reserve the right to modify the itinerary due to unavoidable circumstances such as weather conditions, traffic delays, or changes in local temple trust regulations. However, we assure you that the core essence of the pilgrimage will be maintained.

4. Conduct and Decorum

As this is a religious pilgrimage, all pilgrims are requested to maintain the sanctity of the temples and follow the dress code and guidelines provided by our guides.

Privacy Policy

Divya Ayodhya Darshan values your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic information necessary to facilitate your booking, including your full name, phone number, email address, travel date, and the number of pilgrims.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your booking and confirm your pilgrimage package.
  • To communicate with you via WhatsApp regarding your itinerary and support.
  • To send you booking confirmation codes and payment receipts.

3. Payment Security

All payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI IDs, or net banking credentials on our servers.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Your data is only shared with hotel partners and transport providers strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your booking.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Our cancellation and refund policy is as follows:

1. Cancellation Charges

  • Cancellations made 7 days or more before the travel date: 50% of the total booking amount will be refunded.
  • Cancellations made less than 7 days before the travel date: No refund will be provided.
  • No-shows on the day of travel will not be eligible for any refund.

2. Refund Process

All approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method (credit card, debit card, UPI, etc.) used at the time of booking. The refund process typically takes 5-7 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your bank's policies.

3. Cancellation by Us

In the rare event that Divya Ayodhya Darshan must cancel a tour due to natural disasters, government restrictions, or insufficient bookings, a full 100% refund will be initiated to the customer.
